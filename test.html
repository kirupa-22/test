Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing your young builder for continuous mentorship.

Can my child start directly at Track 2, 3, or 4 if they already know coding?
Yes! While each track has prerequisites (Track 2 requires Track 1; Track 3 requires Tracks 1 & 2; Track 4 requires Tracks 1-3), students with prior knowledge are welcome to test into higher tracks. Our senior mentors will connect with the student to conduct a quick diagnostic session, evaluate their foundation, and ensure they are placed in the ideal track to thrive.
Why is a laptop, active internet connectivity, and basic computer knowledge required?
Software building requires typing structured code, running developer environments, working with React, and testing live web applications. Tablets and smartphones cannot support standard engineering workflows or terminal tools. A functioning laptop with internet connectivity and basic computer familiarity (typing, mouse navigation, web browsing) gives your child authentic engineering capabilities.
How do the free Extended Clarity Sessions work?
If your child faces difficulty or needs extra support on any logic problem or code exercise, we schedule a free 1-hour extended clarity session based on mentor availability. Our mentor provides focused guidance, answers every question patiently, and walks through live examples until the concept is 100% understood.
How does the Referral & Group Offer of ₹1,000/month work?
Form a group of 5 or more students (classmates, cousins, or friends) or refer 5 friends to NeoByte Builders. When registering before midnight on November 15th, every student in your group or referral circle secures the Tech Innovator Add-on for just ₹1,000/month per student!
Can older students, high schoolers, or adults join?
Yes! Our mentorship is tailored from 4th Grade with no upper age limit. We teach authentic computational logic, software engineering, and emerging AI tools that are equally valuable for school students, college undergraduates, and adult lifelong learners.
